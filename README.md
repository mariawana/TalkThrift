About TalkThrift

TalkThrift is our online thrift store where we share handpicked preloved clothes that deserve another chance to be worn and loved.

We started with a simple idea: to make thrift shopping easier, more accessible, and enjoyable. We believe that finding good clothes doesn't always mean buying something brand new. Sometimes, the best pieces are the ones that already have a story

Why I Built This Website

I wanted TalkThrift to have its own space online where customers can browse our available items without having to scroll through different posts just to find what they're looking for

Instead of relying only on social media, i decided to build a website where we could organize our products, show their details, and eventually make the ordering process more convenient

What You Can Find Here

- Handpicked preloved clothing
- Available items with photos, prices, and details
- Product search and categories
- A growing collection of thrift finds
- More shopping features coming soon

Still Improving

TalkThrift is already something we're building as a business, but the website itself is still a work in progress

I'm currently improving the design, mobile navigation, ordering process, and ways for customers to communicate with us

There might still be changes and occasional errors while i work on it, but i'm doing my best to make the experience better

A Little Note From Me

Building this website hasn't been as easy as i initially thought. I've encountered errors, changed designs, and spent time figuring out why certain things weren't working

But i wanted to create something that we could actually use for our business, not just another website that sits unfinished

TalkThrift is something i want to keep growing, both as a business and as a project i'm personally working on
